| 2.1 (dev) | [AwesomeWM compatibility](https://somewm.org/docs/next/reference/deviations/) | [2.0 to 2.1](https://somewm.org/docs/next/getting-started/upgrading-from-2.0/) |

The [2.1 drawing concepts](https://somewm.org/docs/next/concepts/how-somewm-draws/) explain the rendering and layout model.
